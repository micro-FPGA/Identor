**Q: What can I do with my identor number?**
A: You can tag files with it.

**Q: How?**
A: `FILENAME_X_Y_Z.EXT`

   X — identor number of the copyright holder
   Y — year, Anno Greta
   Z — licence. Leave it out and the licence is OLL.

**Q: Example?**
A: `led143_1_7.vhd` — copyright identor #1, year 7 AG, Open Love License.
